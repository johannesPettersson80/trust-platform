# Plant

Plant is truST's library of plant equipment: motors, pumps, fans, drives, heaters, valves,
dampers and cylinders; instruments, totals and tanks; PID, cascade and on/off controllers;
interlocks and permissives; equipment groups; sequences; and alarm and sequence managers. Each
block comes with its symbol, panel and settings for the HMI Builder.
